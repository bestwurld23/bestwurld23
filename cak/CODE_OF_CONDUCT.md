# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are building infrastructure for people who have been shut out of civic participation —
by closed polling places, by ID rules, by distance, by language, by a clerk with an
unreviewable decision. A project with that purpose cannot tolerate the same dynamics
inside its own walls.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
tribal or national citizenship status, immigration status, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Assuming the person on the other end knows something you don't
- Giving and accepting review that is about the work
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, and repairing the harm
- Prioritising what serves the communities this is deployed in over what is interesting
  to build
- Listening to stewards, translators and community partners as domain experts, because
  on the questions that matter here they are

Behaviour that will not be tolerated:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing accessibility, translation or low-bandwidth work as lower-status
- Using this project's spaces to campaign for a candidate or party

The last two are specific to what we build. Accessibility and language work is the
product, not a favour. And a project that resident voters are asked to trust cannot be a
partisan space; take electoral advocacy elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the project maintainers at the contact in `SECURITY.md`. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers must respect
the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers will follow these steps in deciding consequences:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning explaining what was wrong and why. A public
   apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction
   with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A period of no public or private interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violation, harassment of an individual, or
   aggression toward a class of people.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests, chat,
mailing lists — and when an individual is representing the project in public, including
at deployments, partner meetings, and conferences.

# Early voting hours

Early voting runs on weekdays from 8am to 6pm. Early voting is closed on
Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

Early voting begins 15 days before election day and ends 2 days before
election day.

## Election day hours

On election day, polling places are open from 6am to 7pm. Anyone already
standing in line at 7pm may still vote.

## Holiday closures

All early voting locations are closed on the Springfield Founders Day holiday.

# Where to vote

## Springfield Library

The Springfield Library at 1 Main St is an early voting site and an election
day polling place. The building has a step-free entrance and an accessible
voting booth.

Parking is available behind the building. Bus route 4 stops at the front door.

## Riverside Chapel Hall

Riverside Chapel Hall at 22 River Road is an election day polling place only.
It is not open for early voting.

## Finding your assigned polling place

Your assigned election day polling place depends on your precinct. The
Springfield Civic Board publishes the precinct list and can tell you which
polling place is yours.

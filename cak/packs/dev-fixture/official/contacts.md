# Contact the Springfield Civic Board

Phone: 555-0100

Office: 10 Civic Plaza, Springfield. The office is open weekdays from 9am to
5pm.

## What the office can do

The Springfield Civic Board answers questions about registration, polling
places, accepted identification, and voting with a disability. Only the Board
can tell a person whether their registration is on file.

## Language help

Language assistance in Spanish is available by phone at 555-0100 and in person
at the office.

## Accessibility

To request an accessible voting station or curbside voting, call the Board at
555-0100 before election day.

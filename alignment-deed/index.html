Alignment Deed

Read an RFP against your organization. Get a role, a rubric you can argue with, and the funder's own vocabulary.

The opportunity

Paste the RFP, NOFO, or funding announcement. Eligibility and review-criteria sections matter most — paste those if you paste nothing else.

Your organization

The rubric scores the RFP against this profile. Fill it in once.

    Organization profile

    Kept to your account and private. The page cannot crawl your site — the sandbox blocks outbound requests — so paste your About and Programs text and let it fill the fields.

    Paste from your website

    Copy your About, Programs, and Impact pages in. One pass fills everything on the right, which you can then correct.

    Fields

    Compliance readiness

    Saved runs

    Every reading you keep, newest first. Open one to bring it back into the console.

    Shared library

    Opportunities other organizations chose to publish, with no org details attached. Scan it for what is circulating in your issue area.

    How the score works. Claude reads the RFP and your profile and returns findings per axis with quotes. The page then does the arithmetic itself: six weighted axes, a capacity override from your award-to-budget ratio, and a hard blocker capping the total at 34. The role comes from rules on those numbers, not from the model's opinion, so you can argue with any part of it.

    What it will not do. It cannot reach your website or any grant database from here, and it does not verify that a lead is open. Treat the opportunities it names as search starting points and confirm each on the funder's own portal before you plan around it.

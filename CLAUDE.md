### The IBHE question — likely resolved for CEJA, must be confirmed
**105 ILCS 426/30** (Private Business and Vocational Schools Act of 2012)
excludes from the Act workforce preparation programs funded by DCEO under the
**Energy Transition Act** and the Illinois Works Jobs Program Act **where
participants are charged no tuition**. DCEO sends IBHE a list of qualifying
organizations annually.

All three CEJA NOFOs are authorized under the Energy Transition Act (20 ILCS
730), and Climate Works requires "providing training free of charge". So the
licensing gap that killed JTED appears not to apply here — JTED is not an Energy
Transition Act program, which is why it did bite there.

Read from search summaries of the statute, not the statute itself (this sandbox
cannot reach ilga.gov or ibhe.org). **Get it in writing from IBHE and DCEO before
submission.** IBHE's FAQ indicates an entity applies to be declared exempt.
